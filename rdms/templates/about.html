Platform Overview

The {{ UNI }} Result Management & Academic Portal is a full-stack academic information system built to give students, faculty and administrators a single, secure place to manage results, attendance and course delivery — from raw marks entry to SGPA/CGPA computation and downloadable transcripts.

The platform covers multi-role authentication, automated grade computation, attendance tracking with shortage alerts, PDF transcript generation, exam scheduling, and an integrated learning module for course video tutorials and reading material — all backed by an auditable, security-hardened data layer.
